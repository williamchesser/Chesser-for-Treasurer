Meet William

A Life Spent Getting the Details Right

Eighteen years in Washington County public schools. Seven of them in Springdale - Har-Ber, Lakeside, and the Don Tyson School of Innovation, which I helped start. Right now I am the entire high school science department at Fayetteville Virtual Academy. Nine subjects, ninth through twelfth. That is the part of my record I would point to first.

Before that I co-founded a land planning firm here in Fayetteville, Community by Design. I was the COO. Five employees. A $250,000 budget. Land developments entitled in the tens of millions. Before that I was a construction manager on the renovation of the old Fayetteville public library, and tracked $3 million in fleet and equipment.

Alongside teaching, construction, and land planning I have run two small businesses of my own - The Castle Foundry since 2001 and Chesser Design since 2007. Chesser Design is furniture I build myself. The Castle Foundry is an LLC that made toys, took on investors, and worked with factories here and overseas. Side operations, NOT corporations. But you do your own budgets, payroll and taxes, and you find out fast whether you keep good books. I served six years on the Fayetteville Planning Commission, and four years on the city's Board of Adjustment before that. I studied ethics and anthropology at the University of Arkansas. My undergraduate thesis in ethics was called “On Morality.” For a job that is mostly being careful with money that is not yours, I think that fits.

Voters deserve a choice. No candidate should run unopposed. I am running because Washington County's Treasurer should answer to the voters. William Chesser
